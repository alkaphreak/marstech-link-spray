Privacy

What is recorded

When you create a short link, a shortened spray or a paste from this website, sol4.space records, next to the created resource:

  • a hash of your browser fingerprint (the same signals as on the fingerprint page), never the raw signals;
  • your IP address and your browser's User-Agent;
  • the date.

Visits record nothing. A short-link redirect only adds one to that link's click counter and updates its last-click date: no IP, User-Agent or fingerprint. Calls to the API carry no browser fingerprint: the API key already identifies the client application.

Paste, image and abuse report records also keep the IP address of their author for as long as the record exists.

Why

Only to fight abuse: to tie illegal content published on the platform to a signature when it is reported, or when the authorities request it. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the host in keeping the service free of abuse. No advertising, no resale, no cross-referencing with other data.

How long

Signatures are deleted automatically 12 months after the creation. Only the administrator can read them, from an abuse report or a legal request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Québec's Law 25, you can ask to access, correct or delete the data about you, or object to its processing, through the contact form. A fingerprint can be spoofed: it is a clue, never a proof on its own.
